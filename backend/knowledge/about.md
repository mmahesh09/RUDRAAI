# RudraAI — About Us

## Who We Are
RudraAI is an AI automation agency based in Hyderabad, India, founded by Mahesh (also known as Gowri Rudrai). We build production-grade n8n workflows and AI agents for businesses that want to automate operations without hiring large engineering teams.

## Our Mission
We believe automation should be accessible, reliable, and fully owned by the businesses that use it. We build systems that run 24/7, are fully documented, and don't create vendor lock-in.

## What We Offer
We help businesses automate the work that's eating their team's time:
- **Workflow Automation**: n8n-powered automations that connect your entire business stack
- **AI Agent Development**: Custom agents built on GPT-4o, Claude, and open-source LLMs
- **Integration Engineering**: Connect 400+ tools seamlessly
- **Automation Consulting**: Map, prioritize, and roadmap your automation strategy

## Our Approach
We don't build fragile no-code demos. We build production-grade systems:
- Every workflow is stress-tested before going live
- Full documentation and walkthrough included
- You own everything — no subscription, no lock-in
- We stay involved after launch with ongoing monitoring and support
